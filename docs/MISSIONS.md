# Mission catalogue

The offline catalogue contains **176 unique checklist entries** reviewed on November 21, 2026. It covers every named entry in the PowerPyx chapter and Strangers & Freaks index, including the prologue, story interludes, alternate map labels, optional errands and contact assignments. The index lists 191 entries across those two sections. Fifteen show up in both. The atlas gives each overlapping assignment one completion checkbox. ## Objectives and source depth

All 176 cards contain an original, concise player note or objective. **170** link to sources that provide actionable game objectives, a walkthrough of the same task, or a walkthrough covering its contact activity. The other **six** have narrower evidence. Yasmani’s ATM jobs and Dunk’s tips have verified activity descriptions. Nate Will Be in Touch and Heist Plans in Place are short story prompts. Rafa’s Kidnapping and the Chapter 5 fee appear in the mission index, which supplies little detail for those entries. The published Chapter 4 payment amount is attached to the Chapter 4 card. ## Locations and routing

The catalogue supplies an area or location description for 137 entries, a starting character or pairing for 163, and a named contact or other quest giver for 151. There are 37 explicitly named starting venues, including locations used by automatic scenes. The 112 external starting-image references point to 111 distinct published images. Images may show a neighborhood, a marker or a character-specific start. The browser can open the publisher’s image. The source package includes no copy of these walkthrough images.

Three missions share independently mapped starting markers with existing loot guides. Lab Fees shares the duffel-bag guide’s start, Hit Kingpin Bowling shares the watch guide’s start and That’s Where the Money Is shares the café start associated with Yasmani’s tips. A route to one of those markers brings the player to the activity. The activity’s reward can be somewhere else. A chapter, county name or published screenshot by itself provides no atlas coordinates. Phone transfers can happen anywhere and some story scenes begin automatically when the previous mission ends.
